Project Objectives

This 12-week engagement is designed to build a competitive and sustainable compensation framework for Digantara, addressing the critical need for a data-backed, transparent, and defensible pay structure to attract and retain key talent.

Market Alignment

Determine current compensation position relative to the market and key competitors.

Internal Equity

Create standardized compensation bands and a transparent structure for fair pay across job families.

Risk Mitigation

Provide recommendations for market corrections to address pay parity and mitigate flight risk.

2. Project Scope and Methodology (12 Weeks)

This project follows a structured, four-phase approach, culminating in actionable data and financial modeling for corrections.

PHASE 1 (Wks 1-3)

Preparation & Finalization

  • Define & validate distinct job families (e.g., Space Systems, Data Science).
  • Gather current internal compensation data.
  • Determine precise comparator basket for benchmarking.
PHASE 2 (Wks 4-6)

Market Data Benchmarking

  • Execute primary research and acquire specialized market data.
  • Clean, normalize, and analyze data for target positions.
  • Establish competitive compensation benchmarks (50th, 75th percentile).
PHASE 3 (Wks 7-9)

Architecture Design & Mapping

  • Design clear, structured compensation bands (min/mid/max).
  • Map existing team members against new bands and market data.
  • Draft policy guidelines for managing pay movements.
PHASE 4 (Wks 10-12)

Correction & Final Deliverables

  • Develop detailed financial model for market corrections.
  • Provide specific, prioritized adjustment recommendations.
  • Deliver comprehensive final report and presentation to leadership.
